# Context Map

## Contexts

- [Experiment Sessions](./docs/sessions/CONTEXT.md): identifies bounded investigations and their experimental conditions.
- [Dataset Publication](./docs/grilling/push-to-github-preparation/CONTEXT.md): defines the private dataset publication boundary and its evidence.

## Relationships

- Dataset Publication provides locked inputs to Experiment Sessions.
- Experiment Sessions may cite publication evidence, while keeping their plans, specifications, tickets, and results within their own directory.
